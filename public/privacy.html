Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What we store

  • Your public TON wallet address.
  • Your VOLTIX balance, mining sessions and reward history.
  • Who invited you and whom you invited (referrals).
  • A hashed login token so you stay signed in.

What we never collect

We never see or store your seed phrase, private keys or wallet funds. We do not collect your name, email or phone number.

How it is used

Data is used only to run VOLTIX: to calculate rewards, show your history and referral statistics, and to prevent abuse. We do not sell your data.

Storage

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Your browser keeps a login token in local storage; disconnecting the wallet removes it.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete your account data at any time through our official channels.

See also our Terms of Use.
